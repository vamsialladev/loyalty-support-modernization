## 1. Earning points
Members earn 10 points per US dollar spent on the room rate. Points are rounded down to a whole number.

## 2. What does not earn points
Taxes, resort fees and pet fees do not earn points.

## 3. Verifying membership
Always confirm the member number in the guest's profile. Never grant points or tier benefits on a verbal claim alone.
