# Guest Rewards — Missing Points Requests (version 1)

> SYNTHETIC. Fictional policy. Applies to stays whose checkout date (Eastern time) is on or before 2026-06-30.

## 1. Purpose
How support agents handle a member's request for points they believe are missing from a completed stay.

## 2. Which version applies
Use the version of this policy in force on the stay's checkout date, in the property's local time (Eastern).

## 3. Timing
A member may claim missing points no earlier than **30 days** after checkout, and no later than **12 months** after
checkout. Before 30 days, tell the member the date from which they can claim. After 12 months, the claim is declined.

## 4.1 Booking channels
Stays booked directly (hotel website, app, front desk) or by phone earn points. Stays booked through online travel
agencies (OTAs) or travel agents do not earn points.

## 5. Already credited
If the ledger already shows the points for the stay, no adjustment is made. Show the member the posting.

## 6. Approval
Every adjustment needs a supervisor's approval. A supervisor may approve up to 20,000 points per case; above that,
escalate to the loyalty manager.

## 7. Escalation
If the stay record is missing or records disagree, escalate to the property for confirmation. Do not adjust on the
member's documents alone.
