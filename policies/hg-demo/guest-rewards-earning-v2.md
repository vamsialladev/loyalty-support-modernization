# Guest Rewards Loyalty Program — Earning (version 2)

> SYNTHETIC. Same program text as the shared `loyalty_program.md` in stages 1 and 2, effective 2026-01-01.

## 1. Tiers and benefits

| Tier | Nights per year | Late check-out | Welcome amenity | Room upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver | 5+ | 1:00 PM (free) | Bottled water | No |
| Gold | 15+ | 2:00 PM (free) | Drink voucher | Subject to availability |
| Platinum | 40+ | 4:00 PM (guaranteed) | Bottle of wine | Guaranteed one category |

## 2. Earning points
Members earn 10 points per US dollar spent on the room rate. Points are rounded down to a whole number.

## 3. What does not earn points
Taxes, resort fees and pet fees do not earn points.

## 4. Verifying membership
Always confirm the member number in the guest's profile. Never grant tier benefits based on a verbal claim alone.
