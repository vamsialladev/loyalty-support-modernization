## What this shows (written after the run)
- **No failures at any stage:** 4,602 API requests, all 2xx; every investigation that started finished.
- **Headroom against the declared profile:** at `baseline`, 155 investigations finished in 120 seconds (p50 1.7 s).
  The assumed peak is about 45 requests a day (assumption A1). On this laptop, two virtual agents push through in
  about 35 seconds what the whole team would see in a peak day.
- **What breaks first is the app process, not the worker.** Throughput rose from 9.1 to 16.5 requests a second from
  `baseline` to `x4`, then fell to 12.8 at `x10`, while API p95 went from about 100 ms to about 2 s. The queue stayed
  short (at most 16 jobs, the oldest 9 s), so the single worker kept up. The app is one uvicorn process with a
  1-CPU Compose limit, so the likely cause is that one process's CPU. **That is inferred, not measured:** CPU was
  sampled only before and after the run. The fix to try first is more app processes (uvicorn `--workers`, or more
  containers behind a load balancer), then more workers.
- **`/metrics` is the slowest page** (p95 2.6 s at `x10`), because it computes from every stored case on each view
  (ADR-0078). Materialized daily aggregates would fix it when volume calls for it.
- **Postgres wasn't the limit:** 7 to 8 connections, cache hit over 99.7%, no deadlocks.

**Not covered:** the model provider (mock AI), protocol traffic (MCP, A2A), AG-UI streams, a hosted network, more
than one app or worker process, and the customer system's own limits (the mock answers in milliseconds).
