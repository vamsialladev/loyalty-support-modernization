# Problem statement (one page)

> SYNTHETIC history generated from documented assumptions; the numbers demonstrate the analysis method and restate those assumptions. They are not findings about a real operation.

**Who has the problem.** The loyalty operations manager (owner), six support agents and two supervisors (assumed), and the members who never see the tool but carry the cost of a wrong answer.

**What happens today.** 3,872 missing-points requests in 12 months; 27% end with the wrong points; 35% need more than one contact; 96% are resolved within 5 business days.

**What it costs (estimates under stated assumptions).** About $13,078 of points liability over-credited, 6,521,570 points members were owed and didn't get, and about $38,752 of handling time.

**Why.** Stale rule configuration, data defects (duplicate profiles, merged numbers, blank channels, UTC dates), exact-match search across four screens, unreadable free text, and approvals that bind to nothing.

**What we think fixes it (prediction, pre-registered).** About 61% of failures through better tools and corrected rules (no AI); about 21% may need AI for messy requests; about 9% are split between the two. Duplicate credits are stopped by a safe write path for every option.

**Success looks like.** Members end with exactly the points they earned, at the first answer where possible, with no duplicate credit and an audit trail; measured on held-out cases (`docs/hypotheses.md`).

**Not in scope.** Refunds, messages to members, adjustments without a supervisor, the posting pipeline.
