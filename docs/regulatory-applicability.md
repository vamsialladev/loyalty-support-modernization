# Regulatory applicability (ENT-03)

> **Documentation only. Not legal advice. Nothing here was reviewed by a lawyer.** Simulated engagement, synthetic data, so none of these apply to the simulation itself. This lists what a real engagement should ask a lawyer about.

| Area | Why it might apply to a real hotel group | What this project offers | Open question for counsel |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDPR, UK GDPR | EU or UK guests | Export, erasure, retention, minimization | Lawful basis, processor terms, transfers |
| US state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA and others) | US guests | Same tools | Which apply; opt-outs |
| PCI DSS | Card numbers could appear in a message or a folio | Card patterns are redacted before a model. They're **not** removed from stored messages | Whether storing such text brings the system into scope |
| EU AI Act | An AI system used in customer service | Human review, labelling, a system card | Risk class, and transparency duties |
| Consumer protection, loyalty terms | Decisions about points | Citations, human approval, an audit trail | Whether a member can contest, and how |
| Employment and monitoring rules | Staff actions are logged | Purpose-limited audit | Notice to staff |

No certification, compliance or legal conclusion is claimed.
