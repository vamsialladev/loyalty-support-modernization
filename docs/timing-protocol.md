# Human timing protocol (BIZ-02; spec E.7; H7)

> Simulated engagement, synthetic data. **Nothing here has been run.** No participant has taken part, and no human timing exists in this project. H7 stays **not tested** until this runs with real people. Scripted timing never counts as human timing.

## What it answers

Does arm C reduce human handling time per correctly resolved case, compared with arm B? H7's threshold: C's median at or below 80% of B's. With fewer than 6 participants, results are descriptive and the verdict is inconclusive.

## Who

- At least 6 participants. Record each one's experience: loyalty support, this console, AI tools. No names in the results.
- Tell them what's being measured and that it's the software, not them. They can stop at any time.
- Real participants only. Nobody involved in building the arms should be counted.

## Cases

- 12 cases from the validation split, chosen in advance and fixed: 4 controls, 4 unstructured (D16 to D23), 4 others. Never the held-out split.
- Two equivalent sets of 6, matched by scenario, so nobody sees the same case twice.

## Order: counterbalanced

| Participants | First | Second |
|---|---|---|
| Half | Arm B on set 1 | Arm C on set 2 |
| Half | Arm C on set 1 | Arm B on set 2 |

Rotate which set comes first too. Give a short practice case on each arm first, not timed.

## What to record, per case

- Start: the case opens. End: the participant's action is submitted.
- Whether the final outcome was correct, against the label. Participants don't see labels.
- Whether they followed arm C's recommendation, overrode it, and why, in their own words.
- Interruptions: stop the clock, and note it.

## Report

Only measured values: median minutes per correctly resolved case per arm, with every participant's numbers and the sample size. Say how many cases were wrong. Separate statistical difference from practical value, and make no significance claim from a sample this small.

## Limits

Synthetic cases and a small convenience sample. It can't establish customer satisfaction, or what happens under real volume and pressure. The results would apply to these participants on these cases. They wouldn't apply to a real support team until the pilot measures that.
